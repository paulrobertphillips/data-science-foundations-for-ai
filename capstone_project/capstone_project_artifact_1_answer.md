## Capstone — Artifact 1

**Decision statement**
The decision being supported by this system is determining which assests should receive proactive inspection or maintenance in the next planning window, given limited capacity and uncertainty about whether observed signals indicate true failure risk or temporary anomalies.

**Decision owner**
Stakeholders agree the system is directional, not prescriptive. Human engineers are the decision owner and retain final authority over actions.

**Why this decision is risky**
Deployment risk associated with this decision is overperforming proactive inspections or maintenance on organization's assets. Overperformance has the potential to impact organization's annual planning, budget, and resources, which means decision outcome is irreversible.

**What being wrong looks like**
Decision failure would mean missing an asset requiring critical inspection or maintenance services which leads to asset failure and organization incurring costly reactive repairs.
