# 🎓 Capstone — Artifact 4

## **Readiness & Go / No-Go Decision**

*(Modules 6–8)*

> **Goal:** Make a clear, defensible judgment about whether the system you designed **should be allowed to influence real decisions now**.

This is **not** a confidence exercise.
It’s a **risk-acceptance exercise**.

---

## Context (Locked)

You are evaluating the system **as designed in Artifacts 1–3**, without proposing fixes.

* Decision: Prioritize assets for proactive inspection or maintenance
* Strategy: Early-warning, risk-sensitive, human-in-the-loop
* Data: Heuristic, noisy, delayed, heterogeneous
* Mechanism: Rule-based / constrained, not fully automated
* Capacity: Limited inspection and maintenance resources

Assume leadership is asking:

> **“Can we start using this in the next planning cycle?”**

---

## What You Will Produce

You will produce **four sections**, exactly mirroring a real go / no-go review.

---

## Section 1 — Decision Summary (1–2 sentences)

Answer:

* What decision does the system support?
* Why is deploying this decision risky *now*?

This should sound like something said out loud in a meeting.

---

## Section 2 — Readiness Assessment (Five Dimensions)

For **each dimension**, choose one status and justify it in **one sentence**:

* ✅ Ready
* ⚠️ Partially Ready
* ❌ Not Ready

### Dimensions:

1. **Decision readiness**
2. **Data & assumption readiness**
3. **Pipeline coherence**
4. **Human oversight readiness**
5. **Failure awareness**

Your justifications should reference:

* clarity
* alignment
* explicit ownership
* known blind spots

—not performance.

---

## Section 3 — Go / No-Go Call

Make a single, explicit call:

> **Go / Conditional Go / No-Go**

Then justify it in **1–2 sentences**.

There is **no penalty** for choosing *No-Go*.
In safety- or cost-critical systems, it’s often the strongest outcome.

---

## Section 4 — Primary Readiness Gap

If your call is **anything other than “Go”**, answer:

* What is the *single most important* gap preventing readiness?

Do **not** propose solutions.
Name the gap in plain language.

---

## Rules (Important)

❌ No fixes
❌ No monitoring plans
❌ No “we’ll iterate later”
❌ No technical deep dives

✅ Judgment only
✅ Explicit risk ownership
✅ Clear decision framing
