# 🧪 Module 8 Mini-Exercise — Production Readiness Go / No-Go

> **Goal:** Demonstrate that you can make a **clear, defensible readiness judgment** about whether a data science system deserves to influence real-world decisions.

This is **not** a technical exercise.
It’s a **judgment and risk-ownership exercise**.

We’ll continue using your **churn early-warning (Strategy B)** context unless you prefer to switch.

Please review the *e8_1_scenario.md* file found in Module 8's Exercise 1 folder before continuing. This scenario will be used in the following tasks.

---

## Step 1 — Decision Summary (1–2 sentences)

Answer:

* What decision does the system support?
* Why is deploying this decision risky?

Keep it concrete and non-technical.

---

## Step 2 — Readiness Assessment (All Five Dimensions)

For **each dimension**, choose **one status** and justify it in **one sentence**:

* ✅ Ready
* ⚠️ Partially Ready
* ❌ Not Ready

### Dimensions to assess:

1. **Decision readiness**
2. **Data & assumption readiness**
3. **Pipeline coherence**
4. **Human oversight readiness**
5. **Failure awareness**

Your explanation should reference:

* clarity
* alignment
* explicit ownership
  —not performance.

---

## Step 3 — Go / No-Go Call

Make an explicit call:

> **Go / Conditional Go / No-Go**

Then justify it briefly (1–2 sentences).

There is **no penalty** for choosing *No-Go* — in fact, it’s often the strongest answer.

---

## Step 4 — Single Most Important Readiness Gap

If your answer is **anything other than “Go”**, answer:

* What is the *single most important* gap preventing readiness?

Do **not** propose a fix.
Just name the gap clearly.

---

## Rules (Important)

❌ No retraining plans
❌ No monitoring strategies
❌ No “we’ll iterate later” language

✅ Judgment only
✅ Explicit risk ownership
✅ Decision-first reasoning
