# 🧪 Module 5 Mini-Exercise — Metrics **and** Thresholds as Policy

> **Goal:** Make your value judgments explicit by choosing **metrics** and a **threshold strategy**, then rejecting one option with a clear, decision-based reason.

We’ll stay with the same churn context.

---

## Step 1 — Choose a Decision Strategy

Pick **one** and state it explicitly:

* **Strategy A** — conservative, costly interventions, precision-first
* **Strategy B** — early warning, recall-first

---

## Step 2 — Select Evaluation Metrics (2–3)

Choose **2–3 metrics** that align with your strategy.

For **each metric**, answer:

1. **Rewards / punishes:** Which error does this metric favor or discourage?
2. **Hidden failure:** What failure does this metric make easier to miss?

> *Reminder:* Metrics judge behavior on paper; they do **not** decide actions.

---

## Step 3 — Choose a Thresholding Strategy

Select **one** threshold strategy:

* **Risk-tolerance**
* **Capacity-constrained**
* **Cost-based**
* **Tiered (multi-threshold)**

For the chosen strategy, answer:

1. **Constraint enforced:** What real-world constraint does this threshold protect?
2. **Risk accepted:** What risk are you explicitly accepting?

> *Reminder:* Thresholds convert scores into actions.

---

## Step 4 — Explicitly Reject One Option

Reject **one** common metric *or* threshold strategy and complete:

> **“We explicitly reject ___ because ___.”**

Your reason must tie to:

* decision risk
* irreversibility
* trust
* capacity
* governance

❌ No “industry standard”
❌ No accuracy default
