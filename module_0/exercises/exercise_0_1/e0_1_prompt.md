# 🧪 Module 0 Mini-Exercise — Decision-First Framing

## Scenario (our shared baseline)

A subscription company wants to reduce rising customer churn.
Interventions are **limited and costly**, data is **noisy**, and leadership wants “a model.”

---

## **Step 1 — Write the Decision Statement (Your Turn)**

Use **exactly** this template:

> **“We are deciding *[action]* under *[constraints]* with uncertainty about *[unknowns]*.”**

✍️ **Your task:**
Write **one sentence** for this scenario.
Don’t optimize it — just make it honest.

(There is no single correct answer.)

---

## **Step 2 — Stress-Test the Decision (Answer briefly)**

After you write the sentence, answer these **three questions**:

1. **Who acts on this decision?** (human team, system, manager?)
2. **What happens if the decision is wrong?** (cost, trust, missed opportunity?)
3. **Is the decision reversible?** (yes / partially / no)

---

## **Important Rule**

❌ Do **not** mention models, features, or metrics yet.
✅ Stay at the **decision + uncertainty** level only.
